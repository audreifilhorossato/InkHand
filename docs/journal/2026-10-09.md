# 2026-10-09 · Session 2 · Phases 2–3 (electronics and firmware)

Duration: ≈ 2 h 30 min

## Goal
Bench-test the Arduino UNO, CNC Shield V3, 2 × DRV8825 and 2 × NEMA 17 before building the frame.

## Done
1. M0, M1 and M2 jumpers on the X and Y slots → 1/32 microstepping.
2. Drivers seated with the EN pin aligned to EN on the shield.
3. Motor coils identified with a multimeter (Ω): red + green | blue + black.
4. X and Y Vref set to 0.50 V (black probe on the terminal's −, red on the potentiometer).
5. GRBL 1.1f with servo support flashed to the UNO (grbl-servo fork, `grbl-servo-eggbot` ZIP).
6. UGS connected on COM6 at 115200 baud, replied `Grbl 1.1f`.
7. Settings: `$100=$101=160`, `$110=$111=3000`, `$120=$121=300`.
8. `G91 G1 X40 F1000` and `G91 G1 Y40 F1000`: each motor turned exactly one revolution, smoothly, and came back with X−40 / Y−40.

## Measurements
- Motor current: 1.0 A (≈ 60 % of the 1.7 A rating); motor and driver stay cool.
- DRV8825: I = 2 × Vref (R100 sense resistors = 0.1 Ω) → Vref = 0.50 V.
- Steps/mm = (200 × 32) ÷ (20 teeth × 2 mm) = 160.
- One turn of a 20-tooth GT2 pulley = 40 mm.
- Arduino 5 V rail (shield SV/GND pins): OK.

## Problems → fixes
- *Add .ZIP Library* failed in Arduino IDE 2 → copied the `grbl` folder manually to `Documents\Arduino\libraries`.
- Low-memory warning when uploading → normal for GRBL, not an error.
- Sent three `$` commands at once → only `$100` was stored, `$110` got corrupted. GRBL ignores serial while writing to EEPROM. Fix: Soft Reset, then **one command at a time, waiting for `ok`**.
- Command typed but nothing in the console → UGS was paused after the error; disconnect and reconnect.
- Motor didn't turn → the cable was on the **END STOPS** header, then by mistake next to the **5 V/GND** pins. Moved it to the 4-pin header by the X driver. Checked nothing was damaged (ON LED, UGS connects, 5 V OK, motor turns).

## Decisions and why
- 1.0 A instead of the planned 1.5 A (Vref 0.50 V instead of 0.53 V): a pen plotter has a light load, and less current means less heat.
- `$110=3000` and `$120=300` instead of the planned 6000/800: conservative values for an unloaded test. To be revisited during calibration with the mechanics built.
- Rule: **never plug or unplug a motor with the power supply on.**
